mailhub Privacy Policy

mailhub — last updated October 1, 2026

1. Who this applies to

mailhub ("the app") is a self-hosted email application operated by its author, Kiyoshi Sasagawa, for managing his own email accounts. This policy describes how the app accesses, uses, stores and shares the data of the Google Account connected to it. The app's home page is https://mailhub.bktsk.com/.

2. Google user data the app accesses (Gmail)

With the author's consent, the app requests the Google OAuth scope https://mail.google.com/ to access the author's Gmail mailbox over IMAP and SMTP. Through this access the app handles:

  • Message metadata: subject, sender, recipients, date, size, flags (such as read/unread), Gmail labels and thread IDs.
  • Message content: the body and the list of attachments of a message, only when the author opens or searches for it.
  • OAuth tokens needed to connect to Gmail on the author's behalf.

The app does not access Google Contacts, Google Drive, Calendar or any other Google service.

3. How the data is used

  • To show the author his inbox, grouped by sender and sorted by date.
  • To search and display messages the author asks for.
  • To archive messages, mark them as read, or send mail, only when the author asks for it.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling, selling, or training machine-learning models.

4. Storage and retention

  • Message metadata of the inbox is stored in a database on a server the author controls, and is removed when the message leaves the inbox.
  • Message bodies that the author opens are cached for up to 7 days and then deleted.
  • OAuth tokens and passwords are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is kept separately from the database.
  • Access to the app requires the author's own authentication through Cloudflare Access.

5. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties. When the author instructs his own AI assistant through the app, the app returns to that assistant only the information needed to carry out the author's request.

6. Limited Use disclosure

mailhub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Deleting data and revoking access

When an account is removed from the app, its stored data and credentials are deleted. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services; after that the app can no longer access the mailbox.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through GitHub Issues.
